Sunset Vice

Five hands · One night
  • Tap dice to spell a word of 3+ letters. Its cash goes on the table.
  • Bank to keep it, or Roll the dice you have left (never fewer than 4). Every roll adds Heat, and Heat multiplies your words.
  • Roll no word and you bust: the table is gone.
  • The pink die pays double. Use all 8 dice for a Clean Sweep.
